SUPPLIER AGREEMENT
Between: Nexus Financial Group Inc. ("Client") and DataVault Solutions Ltd. ("Supplier")
Effective Date: March 1, 2026

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Supplier agrees to provide cloud-based data storage, backup, and disaster recovery services as detailed in Schedule A. Services must maintain 99.9% uptime. Planned maintenance must be communicated 72 hours in advance.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
2.1 Monthly fee: $28,500 CAD plus applicable taxes, invoiced on the 1st of each month.
2.2 Payment due within 30 days of invoice date.
2.3 Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.
2.4 Disputed invoices must be raised in writing within 10 business days of receipt.

3. DELIVERY OBLIGATIONS
Supplier must provide monthly service reports by the 5th of the following month. Reports must include uptime statistics, incident logs, and capacity utilization. Failure to deliver reports for 2 consecutive months constitutes a material breach.

4. LIABILITY
4.1 Supplier's total liability under this agreement shall not exceed 3 months of fees paid ($85,500 CAD).
4.2 Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.
4.3 Supplier maintains $5,000,000 in professional liability insurance at all times.

5. TERMINATION
5.1 Either party may terminate with 90 days written notice.
5.2 Client may terminate immediately if Supplier experiences a data breach affecting Client data.
5.3 Upon termination, Supplier must return or destroy all Client data within 30 days and provide written certification.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Supplier shall treat all Client data as strictly confidential and shall not share, sell, or use Client data for any purpose other than service delivery.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of British Columbia, Canada.
