────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. TERM
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This Agreement commences on July 1, 2025 and expires on June 30, 2026 (the
"Fixed Term"). At the end of the Fixed Term, this Agreement automatically
converts to a month-to-month tenancy unless either party provides written notice
of termination in accordance with Section 7 below.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. RENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2.1  Monthly Rent: $2,450.00 CAD, due on the 1st day of each calendar month.

2.2  Payment Methods: Electronic funds transfer (EFT) to the account on file,
     cheque payable to "Harbourview Properties Ltd.", or via the online Tenant
     Portal. Cash payments are not accepted.

2.3  Late Fees: Rent received after the 5th of the month is subject to a $50
     administrative late fee. Rent received after the 15th incurs an additional
     $75 fee. The Landlord may, after two (2) consecutive late payments, require
     payment by certified cheque or money order for the remainder of the tenancy.

2.4  NSF Charges: Returned or dishonoured payments incur a $35 NSF charge in
     addition to any applicable bank fees.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.1  The Tenant has paid a security deposit of $2,450.00 (equivalent to one
     month's rent) upon execution of this Agreement.

3.2  The security deposit is held in trust and may be applied by the Landlord
     against unpaid rent, cleaning costs, or damage beyond normal wear and tear
     at the end of the tenancy.

3.3  The security deposit will be returned, with applicable interest, within
     twenty-one (21) days of the Tenant vacating the unit, provided all
     conditions of this Agreement are met. See the Move-Out Procedures document
     for full details on deposit return conditions.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. PETS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1  Pets are permitted subject to the Harbourview Pet Policy (available in the
     Tenant Portal), which forms part of this Agreement. The Tenant must submit
     a completed Pet Registration Form and pay the applicable pet deposit prior
     to bringing any pet into the unit.

4.2  A separate pet deposit of $500.00 per pet is required. This deposit is
     refundable, subject to inspection at move-out, and is in addition to the
     security deposit.

4.3  Unauthorized pets will result in a formal breach notice. Repeated
     violations may lead to eviction proceedings.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1  The Tenant may not sublet the unit, assign this Agreement, or permit any
     person other than those listed on this Agreement to occupy the unit as a
     primary residence without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

5.2  The Landlord may withhold consent on reasonable grounds, including but not
     limited to: the prospective subtenant failing a credit or reference check,
     outstanding rent arrears, or the unit being in an unsatisfactory condition.

5.3  Short-term rental platforms (e.g., Airbnb, VRBO, or similar) are strictly
     prohibited. Any listing of the unit on such platforms constitutes an
     immediate breach of this Agreement.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. RENT INCREASES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1  The Landlord will provide the Tenant with a minimum of three (3) months'
     written notice before any rent increase takes effect.

6.2  Rent increases will not occur more than once in any twelve (12) month
     period. All increases will be in accordance with applicable provincial
     tenancy legislation and any applicable rent control guidelines.

6.3  During the Fixed Term, rent may not be increased. Increases apply only
     upon renewal or once the tenancy has converted to month-to-month.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. NOTICE TO VACATE AND EARLY TERMINATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1  Fixed Term End: If the Tenant does not wish to renew, the Tenant must
     provide written notice at least sixty (60) days before the end of the
     Fixed Term (i.e., by May 1, 2026 at the latest).

7.2  Month-to-Month Termination: Once the tenancy becomes month-to-month,
     either party may end the tenancy by providing sixty (60) days' written
     notice. Notice must be received on or before the 1st of the month in which
     the notice period begins.

7.3  Early Termination by Tenant: If the Tenant wishes to end the tenancy
     before the end of the Fixed Term, the Tenant must:
     (a) Provide a minimum of sixty (60) days' written notice;
     (b) Pay an early termination fee equivalent to two (2) months' rent
         ($4,900.00); and
     (c) Cooperate with showings to prospective replacement tenants.
     The Landlord may, at its sole discretion, waive the early termination fee
     if a suitable replacement tenant is found and approved.

7.4  Early Termination by Landlord: The Landlord may terminate this Agreement
     early only for cause as permitted by applicable tenancy legislation,
     including non-payment of rent, damage to the property, illegal activity,
     or breach of the terms of this Agreement.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8.1  Landlord Responsibilities: The Landlord is responsible for maintaining the
     unit and common areas in a good state of repair, ensuring all structural
     elements, plumbing, heating, ventilation, and appliances supplied with the
     unit are in working order.

8.2  Tenant Responsibilities: The Tenant is responsible for:
     (a) Keeping the unit clean and free of pests;
     (b) Promptly reporting any damage or required repairs via the Tenant Portal
         or by contacting the building office;
     (c) Replacing light bulbs, batteries in smoke detectors, and other minor
         consumable items;
     (d) Not making alterations, installations, or improvements to the unit
         without prior written approval from the Landlord.

8.3  Damage Caused by Tenant: Damage beyond normal wear and tear caused by the
     Tenant, occupants, or guests is the Tenant's financial responsibility and
     will be deducted from the security deposit or invoiced directly.

8.4  Emergency Repairs: For urgent issues affecting health or safety (e.g.,
     water leak, heating failure in winter, security issue), contact the
     24-hour maintenance line at (604) 555-0199.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. UTILITIES AND SERVICES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

9.1  Included in Rent: Water, hot water, building heat (common areas and in-unit
     baseboard heating via building boiler system), garbage removal, and
     recycling services.

9.2  Tenant Responsibility: Electricity (BC Hydro — Tenant must establish
     account in own name), internet/cable, and telephone.
