HARBOURVIEW TOWER — BUILDING RULES AND REGULATIONS

Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Issued by: Harbourview Properties Ltd., Building Management Office

These rules apply to all residents, occupants, and their guests. Compliance
is a condition of your tenancy. Repeated violations may result in formal
breach notices and, where warranted, eviction proceedings.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. QUIET HOURS AND NOISE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1.1  Quiet hours are in effect from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM daily, including
     weekends and holidays. During these hours, residents must keep noise to a
     level that does not disturb neighbouring units.

1.2  Prohibited at all times: prolonged loud music, amplified instruments, heavy
     bass (audible through walls or floors), shouting, and any sound that
     unreasonably interferes with the peaceful enjoyment of other tenants.

1.3  Construction or renovation work within a unit requires prior written
     approval from building management and may only take place Monday to Friday,
     8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

1.4  Residents are responsible for the noise generated by their guests.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. COMMON AREAS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2.1  Lobby: The lobby must remain clear at all times. Bicycles, strollers,
     boxes, and personal belongings may not be stored in the lobby or hallways.

2.2  Fitness Centre (Level 2): Open 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Residents must wipe
     down equipment after use. No outside guests may use the fitness centre.
     Maximum 6 residents at one time. Report damaged equipment to management.

2.3  Rooftop Deck (Level 18): Open 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. No open flames (candles,
     BBQs) unless using the building-supplied propane BBQ stations. Furniture
     may not be rearranged or removed. Noise rules apply strictly.

2.4  Mail Room (Level 1): Residents are responsible for collecting parcels
     promptly. Uncollected parcels held longer than 14 days will be returned to
     sender or donated.

2.5  Bicycle Storage (Parkade Level P1): Designated bicycle racks are available
     for resident use. Bicycles must be registered at the management office.
     Unregistered bicycles may be removed without notice.

2.6  Common areas must not be used as storage spaces at any time.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. PARKING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.1  Each tenancy is assigned one parking stall as listed in the Tenancy
     Agreement. Vehicles must be parked within the boundaries of the assigned
     stall only.

3.2  Parking stalls may not be sub-let, shared, or otherwise assigned to any
     other person without written approval from building management.

3.3  Visitor parking is available in Stalls V1–V12 on Level P1. Visitor
     vehicles may not occupy a visitor stall for more than 48 consecutive hours.
     Overnight guest parking (11:00 PM to 7:00 AM) requires a Guest Parking
     Pass, obtainable from the building concierge or management office.

3.4  Vehicle repairs, oil changes, washing, or maintenance may not be performed
     in the parkade.

3.5  Electric vehicle charging stations are located in Stalls EV1–EV6. These
     are available on a first-come, first-served basis to all residents. A
     dedicated EV stall may be requested through building management (subject
     to availability and additional monthly charge).

3.6  Vehicles without valid registration or that are inoperable may be towed at
     the owner's expense after a 48-hour notice is posted.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. GARBAGE, RECYCLING, AND ORGANICS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1  Garbage chutes are located in the service corridor on each floor. Only
     securely bagged household garbage may be deposited in the chute.

4.2  Recycling bins (blue) and organics bins (green) are located in the
     recycling room adjacent to the garbage chute on each floor. Residents must
     sort waste correctly. Contaminated loads may result in the entire bin being
     disposed of as garbage at the building's expense.

4.3  Oversized items (furniture, appliances, mattresses, electronics) must be
     brought to the Loading Dock (Level P1) during designated Bulk Waste
     Drop-Off hours: Saturdays 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM. Coordinate with building
     management in advance. Bulk items left in hallways, stairwells, or near
     garbage chutes will be removed at the resident's expense.

4.4  Hazardous materials (paint, chemicals, batteries in bulk) must not be
     disposed of in any building receptacle. Drop off at a City of Vancouver
     Hazardous Waste Depot.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. MOVE-IN AND MOVE-OUT PROCEDURES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1  All moves (in or out) must be booked in advance with building management
     by contacting the office at least 72 hours prior to the move date.

5.2  The service elevator must be reserved for the duration of the move.
     Elevator reservations are available Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to
     6:00 PM. Sunday and holiday moves are not permitted except in exceptional
     circumstances and with prior written approval.

5.3  Elevator protection pads must be installed before any items are loaded.
     Pads are available at the management office. A $100 deposit is required
     (refunded upon return of pads in good condition).

5.4  The service entrance (Loading Dock, Level P1) must be used for all moves.
     No moving trucks or large vehicles may block the main entrance or fire lane.

5.5  The Tenant is responsible for any damage to common areas (walls, floors,
     elevator cab) caused during a move. A post-move inspection will be
     conducted by building management.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. GUEST POLICY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1  Residents may have guests stay in their unit for up to fourteen (14)
     consecutive nights. Guests staying longer than 14 consecutive nights, or
     more than 30 nights in any calendar year, must be added to the tenancy
     agreement as an occupant or approved as a subtenant.

6.2  Residents are responsible for the behaviour of their guests throughout the
     building. Violations of building rules by guests will be treated as
     violations by the resident.

6.3  All guests must be accompanied by a resident in common areas unless the
     resident has provided written access authorisation to building management.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. SMOKING AND CANNABIS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1  Harbourview Tower is a smoke-free building. Smoking of any substance
     (tobacco, cannabis, or otherwise) is prohibited inside the building,
     including all units, balconies, stairwells, parkades, and enclosed
     common areas.

7.2  Designated smoking areas are located outside the building's service
     entrance at the rear of the property. Please dispose of cigarette butts
     in the receptacles provided.

7.3  Cannabis consumption (smoking or vaping) follows the same restrictions
     as tobacco. Edibles are not subject to this restriction.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. BALCONIES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8.1  Residents may not store furniture, appliances, garbage, or personal
     belongings on the balcony in a manner visible from outside the building
     or that creates a safety hazard.

8.2  Hanging laundry, rugs, or other items from balcony railings is prohibited.

8.3  BBQ grills (gas or charcoal) are permitted on balconies only with prior
     written approval and subject to applicable fire code regulations. Electric
     grills are permitted without approval.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. GENERAL CONDUCT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

9.1  Residents and their guests must conduct themselves in a manner that respects
     the rights of other residents and staff at all times.

9.2  Hallways and stairwells must be kept clear. Doormats may not extend beyond
     the door threshold. Shoes, strollers, or other items may not be left in
     hallways.

9.3  Any concerns about another resident's compliance with these rules should be
     directed to building management in writing. Residents should not confront
     other residents directly.
