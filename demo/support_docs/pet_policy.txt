HARBOURVIEW PROPERTIES — PET POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Applies To: All residents of Harbourview Tower, 420 Harbour Drive, Vancouver, BC

This Pet Policy forms part of your Tenancy Agreement and is binding on all
residents who own or keep pets. Please read it carefully before bringing any
animal into the building.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. PERMITTED PETS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1.1  The following pets are permitted with prior registration and approval:
     - Domestic dogs (subject to breed and weight restrictions — see Section 3)
     - Domestic cats
     - Caged small animals: hamsters, guinea pigs, gerbils, rabbits (max 2)
     - Pet fish in aquariums (max tank size: 40 litres)
     - Caged birds (non-exotic species, max 2)

1.2  The maximum number of pets per unit is two (2) animals. A unit with two
     dogs, or two cats, or one dog and one cat, has reached the maximum.
     Small caged animals and fish aquariums do not count toward this limit.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. PROHIBITED ANIMALS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2.1  The following are not permitted under any circumstances:
     - Reptiles (snakes, lizards, iguanas, turtles, etc.)
     - Amphibians
     - Ferrets
     - Exotic or wild animals of any kind
     - Venomous animals
     - Farm animals (chickens, ducks, rabbits kept for consumption, etc.)
     - Any animal designated as dangerous, prohibited, or restricted under
       the City of Vancouver Animal Control By-law

2.2  Prohibited dog breeds (see Section 3.2 for full list) are not permitted
     regardless of temperament, training certification, or insurance held.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. DOG REQUIREMENTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.1  Weight Limit: Dogs must not exceed 25 kg (55 lbs) at full adult weight.
     If a puppy is registered and subsequently exceeds this weight, the resident
     will have thirty (30) days to find alternate accommodation for the pet.

3.2  Prohibited Breeds: The following breeds are prohibited regardless of
     mixed heritage, size, or documented behaviour:
     - American Pit Bull Terrier / Staffordshire Bull Terrier (and mixes)
     - Rottweiler (and mixes)
     - Doberman Pinscher (and mixes)
     - Akita
     - Chow Chow
     - Presa Canario (Canary Mastiff)
     - Wolf hybrid or any dog with documented wolf heritage

3.3  Dogs must be on a leash at all times in common areas, including hallways,
     the lobby, the parkade, and the rooftop deck.

3.4  Dogs are not permitted in the Fitness Centre (Level 2) at any time.

3.5  Dogs must use the rear service entrance and service elevator when entering
     or leaving the building. Dogs are not permitted in the main lobby elevator
     unless carried or in an approved pet carrier.

3.6  Residents must clean up after their dogs immediately on building property
     and in the surrounding neighbourhood. Waste bags and disposal bins are
     provided near the rear service entrance.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. PET DEPOSIT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1  A refundable pet deposit of $500.00 per pet (dogs and cats only) is
     required before any pet is brought into the building. This deposit is
     separate from and in addition to the security deposit.

4.2  The pet deposit is held in trust and will be returned, subject to
     inspection, within twenty-one (21) days of the tenancy end date or within
     twenty-one (21) days of written notification that the pet has been
     permanently removed from the unit.

4.3  The pet deposit may be applied toward:
     - Cleaning costs attributable to the presence of the pet (including
       professional carpet cleaning, deodorising, or flea treatment)
     - Repair of any damage caused by the pet beyond normal wear and tear
     - Any costs incurred by the building to address complaints related to
       the pet (e.g., noise complaints requiring a third-party assessment)

4.4  If damage or cleaning costs exceed the pet deposit, the Tenant is
     responsible for the balance.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. PET DAMAGE LIABILITY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1  The Tenant is fully liable for any damage caused by their pet to the unit,
     common areas, or other residents' property.

5.2  If a pet causes damage to another resident's unit (e.g., scratching a
     shared wall or floor, flooding from an overturned water bowl), the
     Tenant is responsible for all repair costs.

5.3  If a pet causes injury to another person or animal on the property, the
     Tenant may be held liable and required to remove the pet immediately.
     Building management may, at its discretion, issue a notice to vacate
     the pet or the tenancy.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1  All pets must be registered with building management before being brought
     into the unit. To register a pet, residents must submit the following to
     the management office:

     (a) Completed Pet Registration Form (available at the management office
         or via the Tenant Portal);
     (b) Current photograph of the pet;
     (c) Proof of current vaccinations (copy of veterinarian records):
         - Dogs: Rabies, Distemper/Parvovirus (DHPP), Bordetella
         - Cats: Rabies, FVRCP
     (d) Proof of current City of Vancouver dog licence (for dogs);
     (e) Signed acknowledgement of this Pet Policy;
     (f) Pet deposit payment.

6.2  Vaccination records must be kept current. Annual renewal documentation
     must be submitted to building management each year.

6.3  Any change in pet (new pet, replacement, removal) must be reported to
     management in writing within seven (7) days.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. COMPLAINTS AND ENFORCEMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1  Noise complaints (excessive barking, whining) will result in a written
     warning. Three (3) written warnings within a twelve-month period may
     result in a notice requiring the pet's removal or termination of tenancy.

7.2  Unregistered pets discovered by building management will result in:
     - Immediate notice to register and pay the pet deposit within 7 days; or
     - A formal breach notice if the pet is prohibited under this policy.

7.3  Residents wishing to report a pet-related concern should contact building
     management in writing. Anonymous complaints will be documented but may
     have limited enforcement ability.
