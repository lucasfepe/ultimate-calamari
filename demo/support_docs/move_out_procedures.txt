HARBOURVIEW PROPERTIES — MOVE-OUT PROCEDURES AND CHECKLIST

Issued by: Harbourview Properties Ltd., Building Management
Applies to: All departing residents of Harbourview Tower

Please review this document carefully at least 60 days before your intended
move-out date. Following these procedures in full is the best way to ensure
the prompt return of your full security deposit.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. NOTICE REQUIREMENTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1.1  Written notice must be provided to building management a minimum of sixty
     (60) days before your intended move-out date.

1.2  Notice must be submitted in writing — email to
     management@harbourviewproperties.ca or via the Tenant Portal. Verbal
     notice is not accepted.

1.3  Notice must be received on or before the 1st of the calendar month for the
     notice period to begin that month. For example:
     - Notice received May 1 → last day of tenancy is June 30.
     - Notice received May 2 → earliest last day is July 31.

1.4  Early termination notices are subject to the additional conditions in
     your Tenancy Agreement (Section 7.3), including the early termination fee.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. PRE-MOVE-OUT INSPECTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2.1  Residents are entitled to a pre-move-out inspection conducted by building
     management. This inspection must be requested at least 14 days before your
     move-out date.

2.2  The purpose of the pre-move-out inspection is to identify any issues that
     would result in deductions from your security deposit. After the
     inspection, management will provide a written list of items that must be
     addressed before move-out.

2.3  Residents who address all items identified in the pre-move-out inspection
     report are less likely to experience deposit deductions.

2.4  To schedule your pre-move-out inspection, contact the management office
     at (604) 555-0191 or via the Tenant Portal.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. CLEANING STANDARDS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The unit must be returned in the same clean condition as it was received. The
following standards apply:

3.1  Kitchen:
     - All appliances (oven, range hood, refrigerator, dishwasher, microwave)
       must be cleaned inside and out, including drip pans and oven racks.
     - Refrigerator must be defrosted and cleaned. Leave door open.
     - Cupboards and drawers wiped clean inside and out.
     - Sink and faucet scrubbed and free of limescale and residue.

3.2  Bathrooms:
     - Toilet, tub/shower, sink, and tiles cleaned and free of soap scum,
       mould, and mildew.
     - Exhaust fan grilles cleaned.
     - Mirrors cleaned and streak-free.

3.3  Floors:
     - All hard floors swept and mopped.
     - Carpeted areas must be professionally steam-cleaned. Proof of
       professional cleaning (dated receipt) must be provided at key return.
       If professional cleaning is not completed, the cost will be deducted
       from the security deposit.

3.4  Walls and Ceilings:
     - Minor nail holes (picture hooks, etc.) are considered normal wear and
       tear and will not be deducted.
     - Larger holes, gouges, or extensive damage must be repaired by the
       resident or the cost of repair will be deducted.
     - Crayon marks, paint scuffs beyond minor wear, or markings left by
       wall-mounted fixtures (TV mounts, shelving) must be addressed.

3.5  Windows and Blinds:
     - Windows cleaned inside (exterior cleaning is management's
       responsibility).
     - Blinds and window coverings dusted and free of damage.

3.6  General:
     - All personal belongings, furniture, and garbage removed from the unit,
       storage locker, and parking stall.
     - Light fixtures clean and functioning (replace any burnt-out bulbs).
     - All surfaces dusted.
     - Professional cleaning is strongly recommended. Building management can
       provide a list of approved cleaning services upon request.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. REPAIRS AND DAMAGE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1  Normal wear and tear is expected and will not result in deductions.
     Normal wear and tear includes: small nail holes, minor scuffs on walls
     from furniture, slight carpet flattening in high-traffic areas.

4.2  The following are examples of damage beyond normal wear and tear that
     will result in deductions:
     - Large holes in walls or drywall damage
     - Broken windows, mirrors, or fixtures
     - Stains on carpet or flooring that cannot be removed by standard cleaning
     - Burns, scratches, or gouges on countertops or hardwood floors
     - Missing or broken closet doors, cabinet doors, or handles
     - Damage caused by unreported water leaks that were not promptly reported

4.3  If the cost of repairs and cleaning exceeds the security deposit, the
     Tenant will be invoiced for the balance. Outstanding amounts will be
     pursued through the appropriate legal channels if unpaid.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. KEY AND ACCESS DEVICE RETURN
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1  All keys, fobs, access cards, parking clickers, and storage locker keys
     must be returned to the management office by 12:00 noon on the last day
     of the tenancy.

5.2  Keys must be returned in person to the management office. A key return
     receipt will be issued.

5.3  Unreturned keys or access devices will result in deductions from the
     security deposit to cover replacement and re-keying costs:
     - Unit key: $75 per key
     - Building fob/access card: $50 per device
     - Parking clicker: $80 per device

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. MOVE-OUT LOGISTICS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1  Book the service elevator through the management office at least 72 hours
     in advance (see Building Rules, Section 5 for full move-out procedures).

6.2  The service entrance (Loading Dock, Level P1) must be used.

6.3  Moving vehicles may not block the main entrance, fire lane, or adjacent
     sidewalk.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. SECURITY DEPOSIT RETURN
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1  The security deposit (and pet deposit, if applicable) will be returned
     within twenty-one (21) days of the last day of the tenancy.

7.2  Any deductions from the deposit will be itemised in writing. An itemised
     statement and any remaining balance will be mailed or e-transferred to the
     forwarding address or bank account provided by the Tenant.

7.3  To ensure prompt return of your deposit, provide your forwarding address
     and preferred payment method (cheque or EFT) to the management office
     before or on your move-out date.

7.4  If the Tenant disagrees with any deductions, the matter may be referred
     to the Residential Tenancy Branch for dispute resolution. Instructions
     for filing a dispute are available at www.gov.bc.ca/landlordtenant.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. MOVE-OUT CHECKLIST (TEAR-OUT GUIDE)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Use this checklist in the days before your move-out:

□  Written notice submitted (60+ days in advance)
□  Pre-move-out inspection scheduled and completed
□  Service elevator booked (72 hours in advance)
□  All personal belongings removed from unit, storage, and parking stall
□  Kitchen appliances cleaned (oven, fridge, dishwasher, microwave, hood)
□  Bathrooms cleaned (toilet, tub/shower, sink, tiles, mirror, exhaust fan)
□  Carpets professionally steam-cleaned (keep receipt)
□  Hard floors swept and mopped
□  Walls checked — patch large holes if needed
□  Blinds and windows cleaned
□  All light bulbs functioning
□  Storage locker cleared and cleaned
□  Forwarding address and deposit return instructions provided to management
□  All keys, fobs, and access devices ready to return by noon on last day
